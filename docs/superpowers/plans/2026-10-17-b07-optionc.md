# B07 Option C Implementation Plan (rev 2): the T-sector of I₂ and the frozen-metric connection reading

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Execute the pre-registered option C design: the κ₁ cross term (Q1), non-algebraic Lorentz-valued curvature (Q2), and the dispersion roots and sign data of the linearized system Υ_lin = 0 (Q3, Q4). It runs at a 4D point, at (3,1) and (1,3), with every §3 control as a hard gate.

**Architecture:** A new package `experiments/B07/optionc/`.
- `sector.py` builds the phased-basis matrices U(k) = iA(k) + κ₁S, N(k) = S⁻¹A(k), G₁, G₃ and S₀, S₁ = iR₁, S₂. It also holds controls 1–5 against Phase 2's `fourier_terms`.
- `spectrum.py` holds the exact characteristic polynomial of N(u), its factor classification, the Q3 kernel data at every root (over Q[ν]/f), the realified Q(√−d) forms (Q4), the determinant identity of control 4, and the backend toys of control 7.
- `torsion.py` holds the 36 = 20 ⊕ 1 ⊕ 15 projectors and Q2.
- `run.py` writes `result.json` atomically after every operator, gates every control, enforces the 2 h per-signature limit, and exits 0 (complete), 2 (failed_control) or 3 (partial).
- Tests (`tests/test_b07_optionc.py`) cover controls only. Outcomes are recorded, never asserted.

**Tech stack:** Python, uv, `fractions.Fraction`, `gu.cliffform.GQ`, python-flint (`fmpq_mat`, `charpoly`, `factor`), sympy (exact resultants, real roots and `gu.inertia`); pytest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-b07-optionc-design.md`, **rev 3.1** (`562370e`). The design is a pre-registration: it was frozen before any named-operator outcome existed, after five review rounds. This plan implements it. **It must not change any outcome class or reading in it.** If an implementer finds a reason to, that is a `FINDING:` for the orchestrator.

## Implementation choices within the design (stated so reviewers can check them)

- **Grade-ordered phased basis.** The 1-forms and 3-forms are ordered `universal.basis(n, deg, g)` for g = 0..4. That makes `universal.rho` block-diagonal. No grade-block reduction is used for the spectrum: the full 64 × 64 N(u) is cheap (design: "if blocks are used"; they are not).
- **The primary spectral object is N(u) on the timelike ray.**
  - With covariance verified, the spacelike ray is checked through χ_{N(e_s)}(iy) = χ_{N(e_t)}(y). This follows from det U(k) = g(k²) and U = S(iN + 1).
  - The null ray is checked by χ_{N(null)} = ν⁶⁴.
  - Neither ray gets its own root analysis.
- **Root classification.** Each irreducible factor f ≠ ν of χ is mapped to h(w²) = numer Res_ν(f, ν² − s)|_{s = −1/w²}. Exact sympy real roots give the massive-type (w² > 0), tachyonic-type (w² < 0) and non-real counts, one per root ν.
- **Rational roots.** For w² = d ∈ Q (the factor ν² + 1/d), kernels and forms are computed on the realification of Q(√−d), and real inertias are halved:
  - the operator a + θb acts as [[A, −dB], [B, A]];
  - the Hermitian form A + θB has real form [[A, −dB], [dB, dA]].
- **Q3 (ii) at every root.** For every irreducible factor f ≠ ν of χ (degree m, exponent e), kernels are computed over K = Q[ν]/f through the regular representation (ν ↦ the companion matrix C of f): a K-matrix Σ_j B_j ν^j acts on Q^{64m} as Σ_j B_j ⊗ C^j, and K-dimensions are Q-dimensions / m.
  - At ω₀ = i/ν, U(ω₀) = −(S/ν)(N − ν), so ker U(ω₀) = ker(N − ν). Also ν²M(ω₀) = −S₀ − νR₁ + ν²S₂. Both are matrices over Q(ν).
  - The data hold for every root of f, since Galois conjugates share kernel dimensions.
  - The kernel gap is labelled `hermitian_radical` when all roots of f are massive-type (real ω₀), and `cross_annihilator` when none are.
  - Partial multiplicities of U are the Jordan block sizes of N at ν. Those of M are recorded only where M is semisimple there; otherwise they read "not computed".
  - **Q4 status per factor:**
    - `computed` for ν² + 1/d (d > 0 rational);
    - `requires_algebraic_extension` for other massive-type factors;
    - `not_applicable` otherwise.

    Where Q4 is computed, its realified kernel dimensions must equal the over-field ones (a gate).
- **Multiplicities.** The multiplicity of det M at a root is recorded as 2 × (the exponent in χ). Covariance is verified first, so u is even. This is also gated: the odd coefficients of χ must vanish.
- **Control 4, the determinant identity.** On each pinned ray:
  - det M(it) = det(−t²S₀ − tR₁ + S₂) is compared with det(2G₃)·det(S)²·det(1 − tN)·det(1 + tN), at the 129 points t = 0..128;
  - det(1 ∓ tN) comes from the coefficients of χ_N;
  - both sides have degree ≤ 128, so agreement at 129 points is the polynomial identity.

  On the null ray, det M must also be constant and equal to det S₂ (control 9).
- **Control 5.**
  - Intertwining and tracelessness, as before.
  - The pairing invariance ρᵀG + Gρ = 0 on 1- and 3-forms.
  - The Hessian covariance equations ρᵀS(k) + S(k)ρ + (d/ds)S(k + sΛk)|₀ = 0 for S₀, R₁ and S₂, at EXTRA_K.
- **Covariance fallback (exception (a)).** If covariance fails, `operator_run` returns only the covariance record and the labelled ray diagnostics of det M on the three pinned rays. No Q1 or Q2 record and no Schur control is computed.
- **Control 6, hand ξ entry.** R with R₁₂₀₃ = 1 must give exactly ξ(R) = ½η₀η₃ e¹²⊗Γ₀Γ₃ = −½ e¹²⊗Γ₀Γ₃ at both signatures.
- **Control 7 as a runtime gate.** `spectrum.backend_controls()` (20 pinned toys) runs before any named operator and again at each auxiliary signature. The toys:
  - scalar waves: massless timelike, massless null (the zero polynomial), and massive;
  - a singular leading coefficient;
  - classification, and the nilpotent degree deficit;
  - both Gram-of-square-symbol toys, realified and over the field;
  - a Jordan chain;
  - a real-eigenvalue root;
  - Q(√−d) Hermitian inertia at d = 1 against `gu.inertia` (imaginary off-diagonals, an off-diagonal pivot, zero directions);
  - a hand-checked non-square d = 2;
  - the conjugation convention, through a restriction;
  - Q_U at d = 2 and at d = ¼, with the check Q_U = ¼V^†M″V = ½V^†S₀V, where S₀ comes from `fourier_terms`.
- **Nested-kernel gates.** On each pinned ray: rank L = 16, AL = 0, S₀·ker A = 0, and nullity S₀ ≥ 16. All are checked before any nested dimension or quotient is reported.
- **Failure handling.**
  - `FailedControl` and `spectrum.ConsistencyFailure` are recorded as `failed_control`, keeping the completed records.
  - A closure or Gram `AssertionError` in `Sector` becomes a `FailedControl`.
  - Any other exception writes status `error` and re-raises.
  - A file left in status `running` is an interrupted run, never a result.
  - Runtime exhaustion (2 h per primary signature, on a monotonic clock) marks that signature `partial` and lists the uncomputed work under `uncomputed`. The deadline is checked at every stage boundary, including after the last operator and before a signature is marked complete. An indivisible stage is not interrupted, so a signature can overrun by at most one stage (a few minutes on the smoke machine) before it becomes `partial`: this is a bounded-grace policy, not a strict wall-clock bound.
  - The final provenance check (clean tree, HEAD unchanged) is inside the error boundary: a late rejection is written as `error` with its reason. Sector closure and Gram assertions become `FailedControl` both in `signature_controls` (the toy) and in `operator_run`.
  - `factor_root_data` records multiplicity_det_M = 2 × exponent only under the precondition that u is even, which the caller gates first.
- **Q4 (ii).** Q_U = (U′V)^†G₃(U′V) = V^†AᵀG₃AV, with U′ = iA.
- **Control 3.** The toy X(β) = e⁰∧β, which exercises S₁, is evaluated at k = e₁ on pinned pairs, directly on dicts, without `fourier_terms`.
- **Control 6.** The B02 responses are pinned: CCC (−1, 0, 0, 0), CAA (−1, 0, ½, 0), CAC (−1, −1, ½, ½), E_OP (2, 0, −1, 0), at odd q with the pinned star.
- **Control 8 at (2,2) and (4,0).**
  - Real closure is decided by `universal.matrix_of` and recorded as a fact.
  - Whatever closure says, the following are checked on raw `fourier_terms`, complex bilinear over Q(i):
    - the transpose identity at k = e₀ + e₁;
    - S₂ = 2(−1)^qG₁;
    - the prime identity;
    - homogeneous reconstruction.
  - The backend toys run there too.
  - Control 1's Hermiticity (`fourier_symmetry`) is checked only if closure holds.
  - Nothing from Q1–Q4 runs there.
- **The 3D toy is not pinned.** Design control 7 says it is not a stop condition until it is. The scalar and Gram-of-square-symbol toys pin the backend.

## Global Constraints

- **P4 exactness.** Exact rationals, Gaussian rationals and exact algebraic numbers (sympy real roots) only. No floats, no modular ranks and no numerical roots as results.
- **Scope tag (P1).** `identity` at the stated 4D point and signatures, for the four named operators, with the frozen metric and quadratic order.
- **Conventions.** Reading V and `experiments/B07/phase2` are unchanged. ξ, ∗ and the brackets come from `gu.shiab` and `gu.cliffform`. The pairing is the pinned one (`second_order.mpair`), with the fixed-X prime convention as a control only.
- **No edits** to existing producing code.
  - New files only: `experiments/B07/optionc/{__init__,sector,spectrum,torsion,run}.py`, `result.json`, `tests/test_b07_optionc.py`, and the Task 3 re-derivation files.
  - Docs: `experiments/B07/optionc/README.md`, `docs/gaps/register.md`, `docs/HANDOFF.md`, `docs/methods.md`.
- **Stop policy (design §3).** Every control is a hard gate. On a failure the runner writes `status: failed_control` with the control's name, keeps the completed records, stops, and exits 2. There are two declared exceptions:
  - covariance failure, which gives ray labels only;
  - real-closure failure at (2,2) and (4,0), which gives the raw complex-bilinear branch.

  Runtime exhaustion gives `partial` (exit 3). It is neither an outcome nor a negative result.
- **Provenance.** The runner refuses a dirty tree over `gu`, `experiments/B02`, `experiments/B07`, `tests`, `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock`, exempting only `experiments/B07/optionc/result.json`. It re-checks HEAD at the end and smokes the serializer first. By design, an untracked README or re-derivation file under `experiments/B07/` (Tasks 3–4) makes a rerun in the main tree refuse; do not "fix" that. Rerun in a clean clone instead.
- **Commits.** On master with explicit pathspecs: `git add -- <paths>`, then `git commit -m … -- <paths>`.
  - Code and tests go in one commit, the result alone, then docs (P8).
  - Trailers: Codex uses `Co-Authored-By: Codex <noreply@openai.com>`; Claude uses its own pair.
  - Tracked files must not link to or mention handoff directories.
- **The orchestrator writes nothing** under `experiments/B07/`, `tests/` or `gu/` while a worker's runner is pending.
- **Wording (design §4–§6).** The following are all forbidden:
  - claiming an EH term, Palatini recovery, a ghost, an instability, a propagator or a "physical degree of freedom" count;
  - calling 𝓔 "Einstein-type";
  - "ill-posed" or "no propagating modes";
  - "the obstruction lives in the cubic structure".

  Also: nullities are "polarization-space dimensions", dimensions name their space, and every sign names its convention.

## Review Focus

1. **The phase congruence.** Πᵀ M_raw Π is bilinear, with no conjugation. A wrong phase must fail the Gram identity (C-F2).
2. **The Hessian factor 2 and the G₃ versus G₁ Gram.** Each must fail the Gram identity (C-F1, C-F4).
3. **The sign of S₁ = i·2(SᵀG₃A − AᵀG₃S).** A swapped order must fail (C-F3). This is the transpose and conjugation trap the reviewers flagged.
4. **The covariance index order.** Λ[l][m] versus Λ[m][l] must fail intertwining (C-F9). The spacelike relation's sign must fail on the real operator (C-F10).

                g = SO2.mpair(x, y, self.eta, "pinned")
                if g.im:
                    raise AssertionError(f"Gram not real at {B[i]}, {B[j]}: {g}")
                if (i == j and g.re not in (1, -1)) or (i != j and g.re):
                    raise AssertionError(f"Gram not diagonal +-1 at {B[i]}, {B[j]}: {g}")
                row.append(Fraction(g.re))
            rows.append(row)
        return mat(rows)

    def A_of(self, k: list) -> fmpq_mat:
        out = fmpq_mat(len(self.B3), len(self.B1))
        for m, c in enumerate(k):
            if c:
                out += self.A[m] * fq(Fraction(c))
        return out

    def N_of(self, k: list) -> fmpq_mat:
        return self.Sinv * self.A_of(k)

    def parts(self, k: list) -> tuple[fmpq_mat, fmpq_mat, fmpq_mat]:
        """(S0(k), R1(k), S2) with S1(k) = i R1(k): all rational."""
        A, S, G = self.A_of(k), self.S, self.G3
        return 2 * A.transpose() * G * A, 2 * (S.transpose() * G * A - A.transpose() * G * S), \
            2 * S.transpose() * G * S

    # ---- comparison with Phase 2's fourier_terms (raw basis variation.one_forms) ----
    def fourier(self, k: list[int], conv: str = "pinned") -> dict:
        """second_order.fourier_terms for this operator, memoized per (k, conv)."""
        key = (tuple(k), conv)
        if key not in self._ft:
            self._ft[key] = SO2.fourier_terms(self.X, self.p, self.q, k, conv)
        return self._ft[key]

    def phased_from_raw(self, raw: dict) -> list[list[GQ]]:
        """Pi^T M_raw Pi in the grade-ordered phased basis (bilinear congruence, no conjugation)."""
        nb, size = 1 << self.n, len(self.B1)
        out = [[GQ() for _ in range(size)] for _ in range(size)]
        pi = [GQ(0, 1) if self.ph[c] else GQ(1) for (_, c) in self.B1]
        ridx = [(f.bit_length() - 1) * nb + c for (f, c) in self.B1]
        back = {r: i for i, r in enumerate(ridx)}
        for (ra, rt), v in raw.items():
            a, t = back[ra], back[rt]
            out[a][t] = v * pi[a] * pi[t]
        return out


def fourier_parts(sec: Sector, X, k: list[int], conv: str = "pinned") -> dict:
    """{"k0","k1","k2"}: Phase 2 fourier_terms' action parts, phased (an independent code path from Sector)."""
    m = sec.fourier(k, conv)
    return {name: sec.phased_from_raw(m["action_" + name]) for name in ("k0", "k1", "k2")}


def gram_identity(sec: Sector, X, k: list[int]) -> bool:
    """Control 4: fourier_terms' (k0, k1, k2) equal (S0, i R1, S2) entrywise."""
    S0, R1, S2 = sec.parts(k)
    f = fourier_parts(sec, X, k)
    want = {"k0": (S0, False), "k1": (R1, True), "k2": (S2, False)}
    for name, (M, imag) in want.items():
        for i, row in enumerate(f[name]):
            for j, v in enumerate(row):
                w = Fraction(int(M[i, j].p), int(M[i, j].q))
                if (v.re, v.im) != ((Fraction(0), w) if imag else (w, Fraction(0))):
                    return False
    return True


def prime_identity(sec: Sector, X, k: list[int]) -> bool:
    """Fixed-X prime convention: M'(k; kappa) = -M(k; -kappa), i.e. k0' = -k0, k1' = k1, k2' = -k2."""
    a, b = fourier_parts(sec, X, k, "pinned"), fourier_parts(sec, X, k, "prime")
    sign = {"k0": -1, "k1": 1, "k2": -1}
    return all(b[nm][i][j] == a[nm][i][j] * GQ(sign[nm])
               for nm in sign for i in range(len(sec.B1)) for j in range(len(sec.B1)))


def reconstruction(sec: Sector, X) -> bool:
    """Control 2: fourier_terms' S0 (degree 2) and S1 (degree 1), fitted on the determining wavevectors, predict
    their values at EXTRA_K; S2 equals its k = 0 value there."""
    n = sec.n
    e = lambda *ix: [int(i in ix) for i in range(n)]
    f1 = {i: fourier_parts(sec, X, e(i)) for i in range(n)}
    f2 = {(i, j): fourier_parts(sec, X, e(i, j)) for i, j in itertools.combinations(range(n), 2)}
    fx = fourier_parts(sec, X, EXTRA_K)
    size, kx = len(sec.B1), EXTRA_K
    for a in range(size):
        for t in range(size):
            s0 = sum((f1[i]["k0"][a][t] * GQ(kx[i] ** 2) for i in range(n)), GQ())
            s0 = s0 + sum(((f2[i, j]["k0"][a][t] - f1[i]["k0"][a][t] - f1[j]["k0"][a][t]) * GQ(kx[i] * kx[j])
                           for i, j in itertools.combinations(range(n), 2)), GQ())
            s1 = sum((f1[i]["k1"][a][t] * GQ(kx[i]) for i in range(n)), GQ())
            if s0 != fx["k0"][a][t] or s1 != fx["k1"][a][t] or fx["k2"][a][t] != f1[0]["k2"][a][t]:
                return False
    return True


def fourier_symmetry(sec: Sector) -> bool:
    """Control 1: S0, S2 symmetric and real, S1 = i R1 antisymmetric (so M(k) = M(-k)^T and conj M(k) = M(-k));
    N rational holds by construction (to_phased raises otherwise)."""
    for m in range(sec.n):
        S0, R1, S2 = sec.parts([int(i == m) for i in range(sec.n)])
        if S0 != S0.transpose() or S2 != S2.transpose() or R1 != -R1.transpose():
            return False
    return True


def rho_full(sec: Sector, deg: int) -> list[fmpq_mat]:
    """The six spin(p,q) generators on the grade-ordered phased basis of `deg`-forms (block diagonal)."""
    blocks = [U.rho(sec.p, sec.q, deg, g, sec.ph) for g in range(sec.n + 1)]
    size, out = sum(len(b[0]) for b in blocks), []
    for J in range(len(blocks[0])):
        M, off = fmpq_mat(size, size), 0
        for b in blocks:
            for i, row in enumerate(b[J]):
                for j, v in enumerate(row):
                    if v:
                        M[off + i, off + j] = fq(v)
            off += len(b[J])
        out.append(M)
    return out


def covariance(sec: Sector) -> dict:
    """Control 5: rho3 A(e_m) - A(e_m) rho1 = sum_l Lam[l][m] A(e_l), rho3 S = S rho1, tr rho = 0 (identity component);
    the invariant-pairing condition rho^T G + G rho = 0 on 1- and 3-forms, and the covariance equations
    rho^T S(k) + S(k) rho + (d/ds) S(k + s Lam k)|_0 = 0 for S0, S1 = i R1 and S2, at EXTRA_K."""
    r1, r3 = rho_full(sec, 1), rho_full(sec, sec.n - 1)
    lam = U.rho(sec.p, sec.q, 1, 0, sec.ph)          # covector action, basis (1 << l, 0)
    ok_A = ok_S = ok_G = ok_eq = True
    k = EXTRA_K
    S0, R1, S2 = sec.parts(k)
    for J in range(len(r1)):
        ok_G &= is_zero(r1[J].transpose() * sec.G1 + sec.G1 * r1[J]) and             is_zero(r3[J].transpose() * sec.G3 + sec.G3 * r3[J])
        v = [sum((Fraction(lam[J][l][m]) * k[m] for m in range(sec.n)), Fraction(0)) for l in range(sec.n)]
        Pv, Pkv = sec.parts(v), sec.parts([a + b for a, b in zip(k, v)])
        d0 = Pkv[0] - S0 - Pv[0]                       # S0 is quadratic: d/ds S0(k + s v)|_0 = S0(k+v) - S0(k) - S0(v)
        ok_eq &= is_zero(r1[J].transpose() * S0 + S0 * r1[J] + d0) and             is_zero(r1[J].transpose() * R1 + R1 * r1[J] + Pv[1]) and is_zero(r1[J].transpose() * S2 + S2 * r1[J])
        ok_S &= r3[J] * sec.S == sec.S * r1[J]
        for m in range(sec.n):
            rhs = fmpq_mat(len(sec.B3), len(sec.B1))
            for l in range(sec.n):
                if lam[J][l][m]:
                    rhs += sec.A[l] * fq(lam[J][l][m])
            ok_A &= r3[J] * sec.A[m] - sec.A[m] * r1[J] == rhs
    traces = all(sum((R[i, i] for i in range(R.nrows())), fmpq(0)) == 0 for R in r1 + r3)
    return {"A_intertwines": bool(ok_A), "star_intertwines": bool(ok_S), "traceless": bool(traces),
            "pairing_invariant": bool(ok_G), "hessian_covariant": bool(ok_eq),
            "covariance_verified": bool(ok_A and ok_S and traces and ok_G and ok_eq)}


def s1_closed_form(sec: Sector, m: int) -> bool:
    """Q1 closed form: S1(e_m)(a, t) = 2 (-1)^(q+1) i [pair(t, X(e^m ^ a)) - pair(a, X(e^m ^ t))] (pair = Tr/2^(n/2))."""
    _, R1, _ = sec.parts([int(i == m) for i in range(sec.n)])
    K = {(1 << m, 0): GQ(1)}
    els = [sec.element(k) for k in sec.B1]
    img = [sec.X(V.wedge_prod(K, x, sec.eta)) for x in els]
    s = 2 * (-1) ** (sec.q + 1)
    for a, xa in enumerate(els):
        for t, xt in enumerate(els):
            v = (V.pair(xt, img[a], sec.eta) - V.pair(xa, img[t], sec.eta)) * GQ(0, s)
            if v != GQ(0, Fraction(int(R1[a, t].p), int(R1[a, t].q))):
                return False
    return True


def direct_hessian(sec: Sector, k: list[int], pairs: list[tuple[int, int]]) -> list[GQ]:
    """Control 3: B(a, t) = 2 <X(-iK ^ a) + *a, X(iK ^ t) + *t> (kappa = 1) evaluated directly on dicts."""
    K = {(1 << m, 0): GQ(c) for m, c in enumerate(k) if c}
    out = []
    for a, t in pairs:
        xa, xt = sec.element(sec.B1[a]), sec.element(sec.B1[t])
        ua = plus(sec.X(scale(V.wedge_prod(K, xa, sec.eta), GQ(0, -1))), SO2.star_c(xa, sec.eta, "pinned"))
        ut = plus(sec.X(scale(V.wedge_prod(K, xt, sec.eta), GQ(0, 1))), SO2.star_c(xt, sec.eta, "pinned"))
        out.append(SO2.mpair(ua, ut, sec.eta, "pinned") * GQ(2))
    return out

def raw_s2_identity(ft, p: int, q: int) -> bool:

"""B07 option C, Q3/Q4: dispersion roots and sign data of the linearized system (design rev 3.1, §4 Q3-Q4).

On a ray k = w u (kappa = 1): det U(w u) = det(*) det(i w N(u) + 1), so the roots are w = i/nu over the nonzero
eigenvalues nu of the rational matrix N(u); w^2 = -1/nu^2. The characteristic polynomial chi(nu) of N(u) is exact
(python-flint) and factored over Q. Each irreducible factor f != nu is classified by the roots of
    h(w2) = numerator of Res_nu(f(nu), nu^2 - s) at s = -1/w2      (one root w2 = w^2 per root nu of f),
as real positive (massive-type, 3c), real negative (tachyonic-type, 3e1) or non-real (3e2), exactly (sympy real roots).
For a rational root w^2 = d > 0 (factor nu^2 + 1/d), kernels and forms live over Q(theta), theta = sqrt(-d). They are
computed on the realification x + theta y -> (x, y): an operator A + theta B acts by [[A, -dB], [B, A]], a Hermitian
form A + theta B (A symmetric, B antisymmetric) has real form [[A, -dB], [dB, dA]], and real inertias are doubled.
Q3 kernel data at every root (any irreducible factor f, degree m) are computed over K = Q[nu]/f through the regular
representation (nu -> the companion matrix of f): a K-matrix sum_j B_j nu^j acts on Q^(64 m) as sum_j B_j (x) C^j, and
K-dimensions are Q-dimensions / m. They hold for every root of f (Galois conjugates).
"""
from __future__ import annotations

from fractions import Fraction

import sympy as sp
from flint import fmpq, fmpq_mat

from gu.inertia import inertia
from experiments.B07.optionc.sector import entries, fq, is_zero, mat

NU, S_, W2 = sp.symbols("nu s w2")


class ConsistencyFailure(AssertionError):
    """An internal consistency control of the spectral backend failed: a hard gate, recorded as failed_control."""


def nullspace(M: fmpq_mat) -> fmpq_mat:
    """Exact rational nullspace basis, as the columns of a matrix (ncols x dim)."""
    R, rank = M.rref()
    n = M.ncols()
    pivots, r = [], 0
    for c in range(n):
        if r < rank and R[r, c] != 0:
            pivots.append(c)
            r += 1
    free = [c for c in range(n) if c not in set(pivots)]
    K = fmpq_mat(n, max(len(free), 1))
    for j, fc in enumerate(free):
        K[fc, j] = 1
        for i, pc in enumerate(pivots):
            K[pc, j] = -R[i, fc]
    return K if free else fmpq_mat(n, 0)


def dim_kernel(M: fmpq_mat) -> int:
    return M.ncols() - M.rank()


def block(rows: list[list[fmpq_mat]]) -> fmpq_mat:
    """Block matrix from equal-size square blocks."""
    n = rows[0][0].nrows()
    out = fmpq_mat(n * len(rows), n * len(rows[0]))
    for bi, r in enumerate(rows):
        for bj, B in enumerate(r):
            for i in range(n):
                for j in range(n):
                    if B[i, j] != 0:
                        out[bi * n + i, bj * n + j] = B[i, j]
    return out


def ident(n: int) -> fmpq_mat:
    I = fmpq_mat(n, n)
    for i in range(n):
        I[i, i] = 1
    return I


def sym_inertia(Q: fmpq_mat) -> tuple[int, int, int]:
    return inertia(sp.Matrix([[sp.Rational(int(Q[i, j].p), int(Q[i, j].q)) for j in range(Q.ncols())]
                              for i in range(Q.nrows())]))


def restricted_inertia(Q: fmpq_mat, K: fmpq_mat, halve: bool) -> list[int] | None:
    if K.ncols() == 0:
        return [0, 0, 0]
    t = sym_inertia(K.transpose() * Q * K)
    if halve:
        if any(x % 2 for x in t):
            raise ConsistencyFailure(f"realified inertia not even: {t}")
        t = tuple(x // 2 for x in t)
    return list(t)


def charpoly_factors(N: fmpq_mat) -> dict:
    """chi(nu) of N, factored over Q: the nilpotent multiplicity and the other irreducible factors."""
    chi = N.charpoly()
    _, facs = chi.factor()
    out = {"degree": chi.degree(), "nilpotent_multiplicity": 0, "factors": []}
    for f, e in facs:
        c = [Fraction(int(x.p), int(x.q)) for x in f.coeffs()]
        if c == [0, 1]:
            out["nilpotent_multiplicity"] = int(e)
        else:
            out["factors"].append({"coeffs": c, "exponent": int(e)})
    return out


def classify_factor(coeffs: list[Fraction]) -> dict:
    """Roots w^2 = -1/nu^2 for the roots nu of one irreducible factor (kappa = 1), exactly."""
    f = sp.Poly(sum(sp.Rational(c.numerator, c.denominator) * NU ** i for i, c in enumerate(coeffs)), NU)
    g = sp.Poly(sp.resultant(f.as_expr(), NU ** 2 - S_, NU), S_)
    h = sp.Poly(sp.numer(sp.together(g.as_expr().subs(S_, -1 / W2))), W2)
    real = sp.real_roots(h)
    pos = [r for r in real if r > 0]
    neg = [r for r in real if r < 0]
    if any(r == 0 for r in real):
        raise ConsistencyFailure("w^2 = 0 root: impossible for kappa != 0 (control 9)")
    return {"factor": str(f.as_expr()), "degree": f.degree(),
            "massive_type": [str(r) for r in pos], "tachyonic_type": [str(r) for r in neg],
            "non_real": h.degree() - len(real),
            "rational_massive": [str(r) for r in pos if r.is_Rational]}


def form_realified(A: fmpq_mat, B: fmpq_mat, dq: fmpq) -> fmpq_mat:
    """Real symmetric form of the Hermitian form A + theta B over Q(theta), theta^2 = -d (A symmetric, B antisymmetric)."""
    return block([[A, -B * dq], [B * dq, A * dq]])


def op_realified(A: fmpq_mat, B: fmpq_mat, dq: fmpq) -> fmpq_mat:
    """Real matrix of the operator A + theta B over Q(theta), theta^2 = -d."""
    return block([[A, -B * dq], [B, A]])


def hermitian_inertia(A: fmpq_mat, B: fmpq_mat, d: Fraction, K: fmpq_mat | None = None) -> list[int]:
    """Inertia of A + theta B (restricted to the realified subspace K, default everything), halved."""
    Q = form_realified(A, B, fq(d))
    return restricted_inertia(Q, K if K is not None else ident(Q.nrows()), True)


def root_data(N: fmpq_mat, S0: fmpq_mat, R1: fmpq_mat, S2: fmpq_mat, AtGA: fmpq_mat, d: Fraction,
              exponent: int, S0_check: fmpq_mat | None = None) -> list[dict]:
    """Q3 (ii) and Q4 (i)-(ii) at the real roots w0 = +-sqrt(d) (d > 0 rational, factor nu^2 + 1/d with `exponent`).
    S0_check: S0(u) from an independent path (fourier_terms) for the check Q_U = (1/4) V^dag M'' V = (1/2) V^dag S0 V."""
    n, dq = N.nrows(), fq(d)
    I = ident(n)
    geom_U = dim_kernel(N * N + I * fq(1 / d))
    if geom_U % 2:
        raise ConsistencyFailure("ker(N^2 + 1/d) has odd dimension")
    geom_U //= 2
    out = []
    for sgn in (1, -1):

        second = restricted_inertia(QU, KU, True)
        rec = {"w0": f"{'' if sgn > 0 else '-'}sqrt({d})", "w0_squared": str(d),

    return out


def kernel_s0_data(S0: fmpq_mat, R1: fmpq_mat, G1: fmpq_mat, L: fmpq_mat, A: fmpq_mat, exact_dim: int = 16) -> dict:
    """Q3 (iii) nested kernels {u (x) lambda} <= ker A(u) <= ker S0(u), and Q4 (iii) restricted forms on ker S0(u).
    `controls` (hard gates in the runner): rank L = exact_dim, A L = 0, S0 ker A = 0, nullity S0 >= exact_dim."""
    KA = nullspace(A)
    ctrl = {"exact_rank": L.rank() == exact_dim, "exact_in_ker_A": is_zero(A * L),
            "ker_A_in_ker_S0": is_zero(S0 * KA) if KA.ncols() else True, "nullity_S0_at_least_exact": dim_kernel(S0) >= exact_dim}
    if not all(ctrl.values()):
        return {"controls": ctrl}
    V0 = nullspace(S0)
    r1 = V0.transpose() * R1 * V0
    herm = sp.Matrix([[sp.I * sp.Rational(int(r1[i, j].p), int(r1[i, j].q)) for j in range(r1.ncols())]
                      for i in range(r1.nrows())]) if V0.ncols() else sp.zeros(0, 0)
    return {"controls": ctrl, "dim_exact_directions": L.rank(),
            "dim_ker_A": dim_kernel(A), "dim_ker_S0": V0.ncols(),
            "quotient_kerA_over_exact": dim_kernel(A) - L.rank(), "quotient_kerS0_over_kerA": V0.ncols() - dim_kernel(A),
            "S1_restricted_inertia": list(inertia(herm)) if V0.ncols() else [0, 0, 0],
            "G1_restricted_inertia": restricted_inertia(G1, V0, False)}


def spacelike_relation(chi_t: list[Fraction], chi_s: list[Fraction]) -> bool:
    """Covariance consequence chi_s(i y) = chi_t(y): odd coefficients vanish and c_t[j] = (-1)^(j/2) c_s[j]."""
    if len(chi_t) != len(chi_s):
        return False
    for j, (ct, cs) in enumerate(zip(chi_t, chi_s)):
        if j % 2 and (ct or cs):
            return False
        if not j % 2 and ct != (-1) ** (j // 2) * cs:
            return False
    return True


def coeffs(N: fmpq_mat) -> list[Fraction]:
    return [Fraction(int(x.p), int(x.q)) for x in N.charpoly().coeffs()]


# ---- Q3 (ii) at every root: exact kernels over K = Q[nu]/f ----
def companion(c: list[Fraction]) -> fmpq_mat:
    """Multiplication by nu on Q[nu]/f in the basis 1, nu, ..., nu^(m-1) (c: coefficients of f, low to high)."""
    c = [Fraction(x) / Fraction(c[-1]) for x in c]
    m = len(c) - 1
    C = fmpq_mat(m, m)
    for j in range(m - 1):
        C[j + 1, j] = 1
    for i in range(m):
        C[i, m - 1] = fq(-c[i])
    return C


def over_field(terms: list[fmpq_mat], C: fmpq_mat) -> fmpq_mat:
    """The K-matrix sum_j terms[j] nu^j as a rational matrix on Q^(rows m) (regular representation)."""
    r, s, m = terms[0].nrows(), terms[0].ncols(), C.nrows()
    pw = [ident(m)]
    for _ in terms[1:]:
        pw.append(pw[-1] * C)
    out = fmpq_mat(r * m, s * m)
    for B, P in zip(terms, pw):
        Pn = [(a, e, P[a, e]) for a in range(m) for e in range(m) if P[a, e] != 0]
        for i in range(r):
            for j in range(s):
                b = B[i, j]
                if b != 0:
                    for a, e, v in Pn:
                        out[i * m + a, j * m + e] = out[i * m + a, j * m + e] + b * v
    return out


def factor_root_data(N: fmpq_mat, S0: fmpq_mat, R1: fmpq_mat, S2: fmpq_mat, cls: dict, coeffs: list[Fraction],
                     exponent: int) -> dict:
    """Q3 (ii) at the roots w0 = i/nu of one irreducible factor f (kappa = 1): U(w0) = -(S/nu)(N - nu), so
    ker U = ker(N - nu); nu^2 M(w0) = -S0 - nu R1 + nu^2 S2. Partial multiplicities of U are the Jordan block sizes of
    N at nu; those of M are recorded only when M is semisimple there ("not computed" otherwise).
    Precondition for multiplicity_det_M = 2 * exponent: u = det U is even (the caller gates it), so the roots i/nu and
    -i/nu of u(w) u(-w) have equal multiplicity."""
    n, m = N.nrows(), len(coeffs) - 1
    C, I = companion(coeffs), ident(n)
    Ub, Mb = over_field([N, I * -1], C), over_field([S0 * -1, R1 * -1, S2], C)
    dims = [dim_kernel(Ub), dim_kernel(Mb)]
    ranks, P = [n * m], Ub
    for j in range(exponent):
        if j:
            P = P * Ub
        ranks.append(P.rank())
    if any(x % m for x in dims + ranks):
        raise ConsistencyFailure("K-dimension not a multiple of [K:Q]")
    dU, dM = dims[0] // m, dims[1] // m
    ranks = [x // m for x in ranks]
    if n - ranks[-1] != exponent:
        raise ConsistencyFailure("generalized eigenspace dimension != exponent in chi")
    ge = [ranks[j - 1] - ranks[j] for j in range(1, exponent + 1)] + [0]     # number of blocks of size >= j
    partial = sorted((j for j in range(1, exponent + 1) for _ in range(ge[j - 1] - ge[j])), reverse=True)
    KU = nullspace(Ub)
    n_real = len(cls["massive_type"])
    reading = ("hermitian_radical" if n_real == m else "cross_annihilator" if n_real == 0
               else "hermitian_radical at the massive-type roots, cross_annihilator at the others")
    return {"factor": cls["factor"], "field_degree": m, "multiplicity_det_U": exponent,
            "multiplicity_det_M": 2 * exponent, "dim_ker_U": dU, "dim_ker_M": dM, "kernel_gap": dM - dU,
            "kernel_gap_reading": reading, "partial_multiplicities_U": partial, "semisimple_U": dU == exponent,
            "semisimple_M": dM == 2 * exponent,
            "partial_multiplicities_M": [1] * (2 * exponent) if dM == 2 * exponent else "not computed",
            "control_ker_U_in_ker_M": is_zero(Mb * KU) if KU.ncols() else True}


def q4_status(cls: dict, coeffs: list[Fraction]) -> str:
    """Which Q4 (i)-(ii) branch a factor's roots take (design Q4, unsupported cases)."""
    if len(coeffs) == 3 and coeffs[1] == 0 and coeffs[2] == 1 and coeffs[0] > 0:     # nu^2 + 1/d
        return "computed"
    return "requires_algebraic_extension" if cls["massive_type"] else "not_applicable"


# ---- control 4: det M(w) = det(2 G3) u(w) u(-w) on a ray, u = det U from chi(N) (an independent path) ----
def det_values(S0: fmpq_mat, R1: fmpq_mat, S2: fmpq_mat, ts: list[int]) -> list[fmpq]:
    """det M(i t) = det(-t^2 S0 - t R1 + S2) at the sample points (M(w) = w^2 S0 + i w R1 + S2 on the ray)."""
    return [(S0 * -(t * t) - R1 * t + S2).det() for t in ts]


def det_identity(S0: fmpq_mat, R1: fmpq_mat, S2: fmpq_mat, N: fmpq_mat, S: fmpq_mat, G3: fmpq_mat) -> bool:
    """Both sides are polynomials of degree <= 2n in t (w = i t), so agreement at 2n + 1 points is the identity.
    u(i t) = det(S) det(1 - t N) = det(S) sum_j chi_j t^(n - j)."""
    n = N.nrows()
    chi = N.charpoly().coeffs()
    ts = list(range(2 * n + 1))
    lhs = det_values(S0, R1, S2, ts)
    c = (G3 * 2).det() * S.det() ** 2
    u = lambda t: sum((chi[j] * fmpq(t) ** (n - j) for j in range(n + 1)), fmpq(0))
    return all(v == c * u(t) * u(-t) for v, t in zip(lhs, ts))


def ray_label(values: list[fmpq]) -> str:
    """Raw ray diagnostic of det M on a sampled ray (more samples than its degree): zero / constant / nonconstant."""
    if all(v == 0 for v in values):
        return "zero_on_sampled_ray"
    return "constant_on_sampled_ray" if all(v == values[0] for v in values) else "nonconstant_on_sampled_ray"


def interpolate(ts: list[int], vals: list[fmpq]) -> list[Fraction]:
    """Exact Newton interpolation; coefficients low to high, trailing zeros dropped."""
    xs, c = [Fraction(t) for t in ts], [Fraction(int(v.p), int(v.q)) for v in vals]
    for j in range(1, len(xs)):
        for i in range(len(xs) - 1, j - 1, -1):
            c[i] = (c[i] - c[i - 1]) / (xs[i] - xs[i - j])
    poly = [Fraction(0)] * len(xs)
    for i in range(len(xs) - 1, -1, -1):          # Horner on the Newton form: poly = poly * (x - xs[i]) + c[i]
        new = [Fraction(0)] * len(xs)
        for k, a in enumerate(poly[:-1]):
            new[k + 1] += a
            new[k] -= a * xs[i]
        new[0] += c[i]
        poly = new
    while poly and poly[-1] == 0:
        poly.pop()
    return poly


# ---- control 7: backend toys with pinned answers (a hard gate, run before any named operator) ----
def gram_toy(G: list[int]):
    """U(w) = C + i w A_U with C = diag(1, -1), A_U = [[0, 1], [1, 0]] (roots w = +-1), Gram G."""
    C, AU, Gm = mat([[1, 0], [0, -1]]), mat([[0, 1], [1, 0]]), mat([[G[0], 0], [0, G[1]]])
    N = C.inv() * AU
    S0 = 2 * AU.transpose() * Gm * AU
    R1 = 2 * (C.transpose() * Gm * AU - AU.transpose() * Gm * C)
    S2 = 2 * C.transpose() * Gm * C
    return N, S0, R1, S2, AU.transpose() * Gm * AU


def first_order_toy(N: list[list], G: list[int]):
    """S = I, A = N, Gram diag(G): (N, S0, R1, S2, A^T G A)."""
    Nm, Gm = mat(N), mat([[G[i] if i == j else 0 for j in range(len(G))] for i in range(len(G))])
    return (Nm, 2 * Nm.transpose() * Gm * Nm, 2 * (Gm * Nm - Nm.transpose() * Gm), 2 * Gm,
            Nm.transpose() * Gm * Nm)

    Ea = [vec1(embed_tensor(H, al, be, p, q), sec) for H in asym for al, be in ((1, 0), (0, 1))]
    g = np.diag(sec.eta)
    return {"E": E, "Ea": Ea, "g": [vec1(embed_tensor(g, 1, 0, p, q), sec), vec1(embed_tensor(g, 0, 1, p, q), sec)]}


def rank_of(vs: list[list[Fraction]]) -> int:
    vs = [v for v in vs if any(v)]
    return mat([[v[i] for v in vs] for i in range(len(vs[0]))]).rank() if vs else 0


def response(sec, R: np.ndarray) -> list[Fraction]:
    return vec1(star(sec.X(xi(R, sec.eta)), sec.eta), sec)


def ccc_inner(sec, R: np.ndarray) -> dict:
    """The CCC inner term: *[B ^ *xi(R)]_C (gu.shiab.shiab_parts' inner['C'] with Phi2 = B), before the outer bracket."""
    return star(bracket(phi_basis(sec.p, sec.q)["B"], star(xi(R, sec.eta), sec.eta), "C", sec.eta), sec.eta)


def q2(sec, P: dict, is_ccc: bool) -> dict:
    """Q2 measurements and the Schur controls for one operator."""
    vs = value_spaces(sec)
    E, Ea, g = vs["E"], vs["Ea"], vs["g"]
    basis15 = [tensor([Fraction(int(P["P15"][r, j].p), int(P["P15"][r, j].q)) for r in range(36)]) for j in range(36)]
    img15 = [response(sec, R) for R in basis15]
    r15, rE, rEa = rank_of(img15), rank_of(E), rank_of(Ea)
    in_E = r15 + rE - rank_of(img15 + E)
    in_Ea = r15 + rEa - rank_of(img15 + Ea)
    R1 = tensor(coords(alt(tensor([Fraction(int(j == COORDS.index(((0, 1), (2, 3))))) for j in range(36)]))))
    y1 = response(sec, R1)
    sol = _solve2(g, y1)
    out = {"rank_15": r15, "dim_15_in_E": in_E, "dim_15_in_Ea": in_Ea,
           "controls": {"dims_E_Ea": [rE, rEa, rank_of(E + Ea)], "schur_15_in_E_plus_Ea":
                        rank_of(img15 + E + Ea) == rank_of(E + Ea), "intersections_sum_to_rank": in_E + in_Ea == r15,
                        "schur_1_in_span_g": sol is not None},
           "one_part": {"rank": int(any(y1)), "coefficients_EF_g_EFprime_g": [str(x) for x in sol] if sol else None}}
    if is_ccc:
        alg = [ccc_inner(sec, R) for R in curvature_basis(N4)]
        inner15 = [vec0(ccc_inner(sec, R), sec) for R in basis15]
        out["ccc_inner_rank_15"] = rank_of(inner15)
        out["controls"]["ccc_inner_zero_on_20_and_1"] = not any(alg) and not ccc_inner(sec, R1)
    return out


def vec0(x: dict, sec) -> list[Fraction]:
    c = U.to_phased(x, sec.ph) if x else {}
    out = [Fraction(0)] * (1 << sec.n)
    for (f, b), v in c.items():
        assert f == 0
        out[b] = v
    return out


def _solve2(g: list[list[Fraction]], y: list[Fraction]):
    """(alpha, beta) with y = alpha g0 + beta g1, or None."""
    import sympy as sp
    M = sp.Matrix([[sp.Rational(g[0][i]), sp.Rational(g[1][i])] for i in range(len(y))])
    try:
        sol, params = M.gauss_jordan_solve(sp.Matrix([sp.Rational(v) for v in y]))
    except ValueError:
        return None
    if params.shape[0]:
        raise AssertionError("E_F(g), E_F'(g) dependent")
    return [Fraction(str(x)) for x in sol]


def hand_xi_entry(p: int, q: int) -> bool:
    """Control 6 (hand xi entry): the off-algebraic R with R_1203 = 1 (pair (1,2) then pair (0,3)) gives exactly
    xi(R) = (1/2) eta_0 eta_3 e^{12} (x) G_0 G_3 = -(1/2) e^{12} (x) G_0 G_3 at (3,1) and (1,3) (eta_0 eta_3 = -1)."""
    from experiments.B07 import variation as V
    from gu.cliffform import GQ
    R = tensor([Fraction(int(c == ((1, 2), (0, 3)))) for c in COORDS])
    return xi(R, V.eta_of(p, q)) == {((1 << 1) | (1 << 2), (1 << 0) | (1 << 3)): GQ(Fraction(-1, 2))}

class Clock:
    """The per-signature deadline (monotonic), checked at every stage boundary, including after the last stage.
    Policy: an indivisible stage (at most a few minutes on the smoke machine) is not interrupted, so a signature can
    overrun by at most one stage before it is marked `partial`."""

def dirty_paths(porcelain_z: str) -> list[str]:
    entries, dirty = iter(porcelain_z.split("\0")), []
    for e in entries:
        if not e:
            continue
        if set(e[:2]) & set("RC"):
            src = next(entries)
            if src != OUT_REL:
                dirty.append(src)
        if e[3:] != OUT_REL:
            dirty.append(e[3:])
    return dirty


def git_commit() -> str:

    dirty = dirty_paths(out)
    if dirty:
        raise SystemExit("uncommitted producing code or inputs:\n" + "\n".join(dirty))

def signature_controls(p: int, q: int) -> tuple[dict, dict]:

    e = lambda *ix: [int(i in ix) for i in range(n)]
    # controls 1, 2, 4, 6 (operator-independent ones in signature_controls; backend 7 before any operator)
    gate(f"{name}: fourier_symmetry", S.fourier_symmetry(sec))
    gate(f"{name}: reconstruction", S.reconstruction(sec, X))
    for k in [e(i) for i in range(n)] + [e(i, j) for i in range(n) for j in range(i + 1, n)] + [S.EXTRA_K]:
        gate(f"{name}: gram_identity {k}", S.gram_identity(sec, X, k))
    gate(f"{name}: S2 = 2(-1)^q G1", sec.parts([0] * n)[2] == sec.G1 * 2 * (-1) ** q)
    gate(f"{name}: prime_identity", S.prime_identity(sec, X, e(0, 1)))
    gate(f"{name}: s1_closed_form", all(S.s1_closed_form(sec, m) for m in range(n)))
    resp = [str(x) for x in G.response(X, p, q)]
    gate(f"{name}: B02 response", resp == B02_RESPONSE[name])
    for r, u in rays.items():
        S0, R1, S2 = sec.parts(u)

        return {"operator": name, "response": resp, "Q3_Q4": {"covariance": cov, "ray_diagnostics": diag}}

    S0, R1, S2 = sec.parts(rays["null"])

def _nonzero(M):
    return [(i, j) for i in range(M.nrows()) for j in range(M.ncols()) if M[i, j] != 0]


def aux_controls(p: int, q: int, names=S.OPERATORS) -> dict:
    """Control 8 at (2,2)/(4,0): star signs, the backend toys, and per operator real closure (a recorded fact), the
    transpose identity, S2 = 2(-1)^q G1, the prime identity and homogeneous reconstruction, all on raw fourier_terms
    (complex bilinear over Q(i), valid whatever closure says); control 1's Hermiticity only if closure holds."""
    out = {"signature": [p, q], "star_adjoint_sign_1": SO2.star_adjoint_sign(p, q, 1, "pinned"),
           "mass_sign": SO2.mass_sign(p, q, "pinned")}
    gate(f"({p},{q}) mass sign", out["mass_sign"] == (-1) ** q)
    out["backend_controls"] = all(SP.backend_controls().values())
    gate(f"({p},{q}) backend controls", out["backend_controls"])
    out["operators"] = {}
    for name in names:
        X = S.operator(name, p, q)
        memo: dict = {}

        def ft(k, conv, X=X, memo=memo):
            key = (tuple(k), conv)
            if key not in memo:
                memo[key] = SO2.fourier_terms(X, p, q, list(k), conv)
            return memo[key]
        try:
            U.matrix_of(X, p, q)
            closed = True
        except AssertionError:
            closed = False
        rec = {"real_closure": closed}
        m = ft((1, 1, 0, 0), "pinned")
        rec["transpose_identity"] = _sym(m["action_k0"], 1) and _sym(m["action_k1"], -1) and _sym(m["action_k2"], 1)
        rec["S2_identity"] = S.raw_s2_identity(ft, p, q)
        rec["prime_identity"] = S.raw_prime_identity(ft, (1, 1, 0, 0))
        rec["reconstruction"] = S.raw_reconstruction(ft, p + q)
        for k in ("transpose_identity", "S2_identity", "prime_identity", "reconstruction"):
            gate(f"({p},{q}) {name}: {k}", rec[k])
        if closed:
            rec["fourier_symmetry"] = S.fourier_symmetry(S.Sector(X, p, q))
            gate(f"({p},{q}) {name}: fourier_symmetry", rec["fourier_symmetry"])
        out["operators"][name] = rec
    return out


def _sym(m: dict, s: int) -> bool:
    return all(m.get((t, a), V.GQ()) == v * V.GQ(s) for (a, t), v in m.items())


def run(path: Path = OUT, provenance: bool = True, deadline_s: float = DEADLINE_S) -> int:
    commit = git_commit() if provenance else "no-provenance (test)"
    json.dumps(jsonable({"smoke": Fraction(1, 2)}))
    t = time.time()
    out = {"scope": SCOPE, "source_commit": commit, "design": DESIGN, "status": "running", "signatures": []}
    todo = [f"({p},{q}) {nm}" for p, q in S.SIGS for nm in S.OPERATORS] + [f"({p},{q}) auxiliary" for p, q in S.AUX_SIGS]
    out["uncomputed"] = list(todo)
    write_result(out, path)
    try:
        out["backend_controls"] = SP.backend_controls()
        gate("backend controls (control 7)", all(out["backend_controls"].values()))
        for p, q in S.SIGS:
            clock = Clock(deadline_s)
            sig = {"signature": [p, q], "status": "running", "operators": []}
            out["signatures"].append(sig)
            try:
                sig["controls"], P = signature_controls(p, q)
                for nm in S.OPERATORS:
                    clock.tick(f"({p},{q}) {nm}")
                    sig["operators"].append(operator_run(nm, p, q, P, clock))
                    out["uncomputed"].remove(f"({p},{q}) {nm}")
                    write_result(out, path)
                    clock.tick(f"({p},{q}) after {nm}")
                sig["status"] = "complete"
            except Deadline as exc:
                sig["status"], sig["deadline_hit_at"] = "partial", str(exc)
        out["auxiliary"] = []
        for p, q in S.AUX_SIGS:
            out["auxiliary"].append(aux_controls(p, q))
            out["uncomputed"].remove(f"({p},{q}) auxiliary")
            write_result(out, path)
        done = not out["uncomputed"] and all(s["status"] == "complete" for s in out["signatures"])
        out["status"] = "complete" if done else "partial"
    except (FailedControl, SP.ConsistencyFailure) as exc:
        out["status"], out["failed_control"] = "failed_control", f"{type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"
    except BaseException as exc:
        out["status"], out["error"] = "error", repr(exc)
        out["seconds"] = round(time.time() - t, 1)
        write_result(out, path)
        raise
    out["seconds"] = round(time.time() - t, 1)
    if provenance:                            # final provenance inside the error-serialization boundary
        try:
            moved = git_commit() != commit
        except BaseException as exc:
            out["status"], out["error"] = "error", f"final provenance check: {exc}"
            write_result(out, path)
            raise
        if moved:
            out["status"], out["error"] = "error", "final provenance check: HEAD moved during the run"
            write_result(out, path)
            raise SystemExit("HEAD moved during the run")
    write_result(out, path)
    print(f"wrote {path} ({out['status']})")
    return {"complete": 0, "failed_control": 2, "partial": 3}[out["status"]]


def main() -> None:
    raise SystemExit(run())


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
