## Independence and conventions

The only project imports are `gammas` and `dirac_pairing` from `gu.clifford`. The implementation uses sparse actual matrix entries over Q(i), with exact python-flint rationals. Matrix multiplication is ordinary sparse row/column multiplication, not Clifford-mask multiplication. Gamma products form the coordinate basis. Coordinates are extracted as `Tr(G_C^-1 M)/dim(S)`; all basis matrices are unitary, so the inverse is the conjugate transpose. Permutation-support grouping skips only provably zero traces. Every extraction rebuilds the entire input matrix and checks exact equality, including absent coordinates.

Formula sources were the plan's mathematics section, B02 transcription/proofs/README, escape derivations E2, phase2 README/derivations, and `gu/clifford.py`. The producing implementations and earlier independent re-derivations were not read. All 32 inputs and blades are generated from the specification; comparison data is loaded by the script only after all outputs are computed.

The curvature has sectional values +1 on a0a1/a2a3 and -1 on a0a2/a1a3, with eta factors. Its curvature symmetries, first Bianchi identity and vanishing Ricci contraction are checked exactly. Xi uses one half and both ordered-pair ranges a<b, c<d, raising the Clifford pair with eta_c eta_d. The Hodge star is sigma(I,I-complement) times the product of eta_i on I. C is the first phase of the volume product whose matrix is Hermitian and squares to identity, following the allowed gamma source. CCC/CAA use F,B; CAC uses F+F-prime,B-prime, with B-prime=i B C. The bracket is the coefficient commutator or i times the coefficient anticommutator, with exterior wedge on forms; all three positions and the -1/2 nested term of (9.3) are evaluated directly.

The dressing phase is selected through `u^dagger A + A u = 0`, and `h^dagger A h = A` is checked as an actual matrix equality. The Cayley equation and exact inverse are also checked. For each bivector, evenness, reverse norm one and vector preservation are checked; membership in the identity component uses E2.3's continuous Cayley path and the positive denominator on 0<=t<=1/2.

The covariance control evaluates the dressed operator on `h^-1 x h` directly; it is not assigned zero from the covariance theorem. Transport membership instead evaluates `h x h^-1`, extracts all Clifford grades by trace, requires real pure bivectors, inverts xi, rebuilds the matrix-valued form, scales the recovered tensor to integers, and checks all algebraic-curvature equations and Ricci-flatness. `commutes` checks the commutator of h with each coefficient of xi(W).
